Scenario limits and assumptions

The borrow illustration assumes NVDAB is the only collateral, the entered target is the full debt, no accrued interest, and no E-Mode. The displayed minimum uses the current collateral factor without a safety buffer or fees; it is not a recommended deposit. It excludes other positions and protocol protections. Venus API values are indexed snapshots that can lag chain state and aren't authoritative for live prices, permissions, pause state, borrowing availability or liquidation safety. Indexed pool cash and nominal capacity do not guarantee a borrow. Verify current contract state before acting.

# Set and Earn: separate qualification program

Last verified: 2026-10-05. Scope: November 2026 campaign, not this repository's submission. Canonical owner/source: [official campaign](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/hackathons/smart-money-era-set-and-earn), checked 2026-10-05, and the separate Set and Earn repository's dated rules audit. Supersedes: none. Status: CURRENT, with explicit unknowns below.

Window ends **2026-11-05 12:00 UTC**. The campaign is for a limited merchandise drop, not the Tokenized Stocks cash prize. One registered campaign wallet per person must perform qualifying actions after registration. The participant must hire **three different agents across at least two shortlisted marketplaces**, with on-chain hire events. The built agent must be owned by that registered wallet, registered under **ERC-8004 on chain 56 or 97**, listed on a shortlisted marketplace, have a resolvable public agent card declaring its task and one of **yield, grid, rebalancing or health factor**, and respond when probed. Its public repository must expose its registry ID and chain.

The built agent also needs **three completed hires from three distinct independent external wallets**, none controlled or funded by the builder, plus **at least five category-consistent on-chain actions across three separate days**. A read-only safety agent does **not** meet the execution requirement. No Sybil wallets, common funding, circular or reciprocal fake hiring, cosmetic duplicates or fake on-chain actions. [Official rules](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/hackathons/smart-money-era-set-and-earn) govern verification.

Current qualification state in this repository: **UNKNOWN / NOT QUALIFIED**. No public evidence here establishes an ERC-8004 identity, live listed agent, independent hires or qualifying actions. The separate campaign repository tracks its own wallet and operations; do not copy its secrets here. The official rules explicitly allow participant hires on testnet or mainnet; whether the built agent's three external hires and five actions may qualify on testnet remains unconfirmed. Reverify this point against the [official rules](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/hackathons/smart-money-era-set-and-earn) before spending.

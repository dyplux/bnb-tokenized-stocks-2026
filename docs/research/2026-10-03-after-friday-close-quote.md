# Binance Web3 quote after Friday's late US equity session

**Observed:** Saturday 2026-10-03 00:02:00.223 UTC, Friday 2026-10-02 20:02:00.223 New York time. **Method:** the [predefined one-call protocol](2026-10-03-weekend-quote-protocol.md) and the [read-only probe](../../scripts/probe_binance_quote.py). The [Nasdaq published-hours page](https://www.nasdaq.com/23-5-trading), checked 2026-10-02, described its current 04:00 to 20:00 Eastern schedule. The call was two minutes after that published late-session end. This is one clock-time observation, not an audit of every traditional trading venue.

The probe sent one signed Binance Web3 `GET /api/v1/dex/aggregator/quote` on BNB Chain 56 for exactly `1000000000000000000` raw NVDAB units to the pinned USDT contract. It used a fresh temporary nonholder address. The response had HTTP 200, business code 0 and one `LiquidMesh` route with `executionMode=SWAP`. The request took 801.055 ms. Its raw estimated USDT output was `234581506161816758656`, or **234.581506161816758656 USDT** using the previously checked 18-decimal USDT metadata. The quote's `tradeFee` was `0.01783791`, labelled by the [Trading API reference](https://web3.binance.com/en/dev-docs/catalog/web3-wallet/api/rest-api/trading-api) as an estimated network fee in USD. `estimateGasFee=450000` was also present. A prior [unsigned build](2026-10-02-first-live-swap-build.md) showed this field matching a gas limit for one route; neither field is a paid cost in this call. Token decimals were not rechecked atomically with this quote.

The sanitized result was reviewed locally before this note. The temporary address, credential, signed headers, quote ID and raw response are not stored in the repository. There was no approval, wallet signature, transaction build, simulation, broadcast or trade in this observation. The response establishes that one vendor returned an estimate shortly after the published Friday late session ended. It doesn't establish holder eligibility, an executable sale, a fill, net proceeds, an access advantage over every broker, a price discrepancy or a reason to borrow instead. The earlier [17:15 New York quote](2026-10-02-after-regular-close-quote.md) is another dated observation, not a matched price control.

# Issuer access gate before a funded stock-token demo

**Checked:** 2026-10-04 UTC. **Scope:** self-custodial BNB Chain route, not access through a Binance exchange account. A read-only Trading API quote has returned for several bStocks, but no eligible holder, funded order, fill or transfer has been verified.

Binance's [bStocks FAQ](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/f0c03cd6509a4085b4cce1636f16be38) says third-party integrations must enforce geographic restrictions and refers to a public country-eligibility REST endpoint. The same FAQ says bStocks can face smart-contract transfer controls, eligibility checks and restricted wallets. [The deposit and conversion FAQ](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/f0d41139fadc4790bf9a4c0c7bce2e88) repeats that integrators should check jurisdiction. Its page doesn't supply the endpoint URL or a method to determine an individual wallet's eligibility. A bounded 4 October search of the public FAQs and Binance Web3 developer references didn't locate a callable country-eligibility path. A direct request for the developer documentation bundle was blocked by a CloudFront challenge, so **endpoint availability remains unverified**, not disproved.

This matters for the demo budget: a quoted route from a randomly generated, unfunded address is evidence that a vendor offered an estimate at that moment. It doesn't establish that a founder in Portugal is allowed to buy, hold, transfer or sell the issuer's token. Portugal isn't adjudicated by this note. Don't fund or execute a bStock trade as a substitute for checking the current issuer and marketplace conditions.

## Ondo NVDAon: conflicting public eligibility descriptions

**Checked 2026-10-04 UTC.** Ondo's [eligibility documentation](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/eligibility) lists any EEA member state under a professional-client or qualified-investor restriction for issuance, with separate issuer KYC conditions. Its [Binance launch announcement](https://ondo.finance/blog/tokenized-stocks-live-on-binance), however, says the EEA offer includes professional and retail investors. The indexed [Ondo asset-page disclosure](https://app.ondo.finance/assets/vtion) says a prospectus was notified to certain EEA states and many, but not all, Ondo Stocks are available to retail investors. These public statements differ in scope and may reflect asset-specific offering documents or changes over time. The [NVDAon page](https://app.ondo.finance/assets/nvdaon) did not expose its final terms and KID to our text fetch, so neither Portugal's notification status nor this token's retail classification has been verified here. The general page and a successful route quote cannot settle that question.

**Operational consequence:** keep `eligibility_status=UNKNOWN` for NVDAon. Before a funded NVDAon action, obtain the current NVDAon final terms, KID, relevant EEA notification and venue terms from the issuer's official page, and establish the intended user's actual eligibility. A separate marketplace route may have additional restrictions. Do not treat the absence of Portugal on Ondo's prohibition list as proof of access.

**Next gate:** obtain the exact official endpoint or issuer guidance, record its timestamp and result for the intended jurisdiction and asset, then check the actual wallet's gas, amount, allowance, simulation and loss ceiling before any mainnet action. If this access gate cannot be verified before the deadline, keep the demo read-only and state that limit in the submission. Each Ondo or xStock alternative needs its own issuer-access check; it doesn't inherit bStock eligibility.

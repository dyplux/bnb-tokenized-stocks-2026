# Eligible holder cash-task observation protocol

**Prepared:** 2026-10-01. **Sessions completed:** zero. This is a plan for observing a real decision, not an interview result or demand claim.

## Participant and consent

Ask one person who already holds a bStock on BNB Chain and independently confirms they may use the relevant services in their jurisdiction. Do not record their name, country, wallet address, holdings, screenshots of personal positions or credentials in this repo. Participation is voluntary. No purchase, sale, deposit, borrow, wallet signature or spending is needed for this research session. If no eligible holder is available, record that gap and use a clearly labelled scenario for the demo.

## Task, before showing Dyplux

Ask: “Suppose you need a chosen amount of USDT from this holding this week. What would you do first, and what information would make you switch between selling part and borrowing against it?” Use the participant's own task and amount if they offer one; otherwise use a hypothetical amount and label it as such. Ask them to try the existing sale route and the Venus borrowing flow, or describe why they cannot. Note which costs, risks and units they can actually see, the source and time, and any point where they stop. Do not suggest the proposed answer.

## Same-task product comparison

Show the current Dyplux read-only interface. Let the participant enter the same cash target and NVDAB units. If a signed Binance quote is available, disclose its capture time, route and limits; if not, leave sale proceeds unknown. The Venus figures stay a market-level scenario until account-wide state can be verified. Ask the participant to state their next action and the evidence behind it. Do not describe the app as recommending a trade.

## Record and decision

In the private [DX field log](../dx/field-log.md), record only consent/eligibility confirmation, UTC session time, asset, amount band, incumbent path and observed obstacle, app state, participant's stated next action, whether the app changed that action, and why. Mark hypothetical answers separately. One session is a task observation, not market demand. Retire or redesign D-017 if the incumbent already gives the same decision with equal clarity, if the proposed comparison leaves costs or risk unanswerable, or if no eligible holder task can be observed before submission.

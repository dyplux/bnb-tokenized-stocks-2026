# Live BNB Chain RWA catalog and Sunday quote checks

**Observed:** 2026-10-04 10:22 to 11:10 UTC for the initial snapshot, with a 11:40 UTC aligned-window update. All Binance Web3 calls were signed, read-only, logged through `scripts/rwa_research.py`, and made from the private project credential. No wallet signature, build, broadcast or position was used. The wallet field for quotes was an ephemeral nonholder address, not recorded in the output.

## Catalog and market-hours tape

The signed Binance Web3 [`/rwa/tokens`](https://web3.binance.com/en/dev-docs/catalog/web3-wallet/api/rest-api/rwa-data) returned 488 BNB Chain entries: 442 Ondo and 46 bStock. A separate public Binance website list returned 130 BNB Chain xStock entries. The merged [catalog](../../data/normalized/rwa_catalog.json) has 618 rows and a Parquet twin. The [normalized groups](../../data/normalized/canonical_securities.json) contain 492 provisional ticker-plus-asset-type candidates and 102 with more than one provider. There are 618 distinct contract representations. Those groups don't establish legal or economic equivalence. The public xStock `multiplier` field is retained raw, with no verified token/share semantics.

A bounded [xStock price probe](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-001/xstock_price_probe_results.json) sent the 130 listed contracts to signed `/rwa/price` in four batches of at most 35. All 130 returned a row, but 77 had null price and timestamp. Of 53 rows with a token-price timestamp, 36 were older than seven days and only two were under one hour old at 10:56 UTC on Sunday. The median age among those 53 was 404.63 hours. `platformId` was null on all 130 price rows, so provider identity still comes from the public listing. These token timestamps say nothing about an independent underlying-equity reference. The 100-address batch documented as allowed returned HTTP 414, described in the [DevEx repro](../devex/repros/2026-10-04-rwa-price-url-limit.md). This coverage and quality result doesn't justify adding all xStocks to the five-minute collector; a targeted subset can be revisited after product selection.

The live [market-hours tape](../../data/market_hours/) began with nine selected contracts for AAPL, NVDA, TSLA, COIN and MSTR. The nine-contract scope was an intentional query filter, not an API limit. At 10:43 UTC it expanded to all 35 bStock equities plus those five Ondo equities, 40 contracts in one price batch against the API's documented 100-address limit. At 10:50 UTC the tape held 156 unique live rows, with 40 from that cycle. Each row labels `LIVE`, source hashes and missing fields. The 3 October Saturday gap hasn't been backfilled. In a full 488-row signed response, 31 Ondo entries reported `marketStatus=offhours`, an enum absent from the [current RWA docs](https://web3.binance.com/en/dev-docs/catalog/web3-wallet/api/rest-api/rwa-data); see the [raw-response repro](../devex/repros/2026-10-04-market-status-enum.md).

The API's `tokenPriceUpdatedAt` permits token-price age measurement. In all tape rows, `reference_price_updated_at=null`, `reference_age_seconds=null` and `reference_age_status=UNKNOWN`. The signed `/rwa/price` response has no independent reference timestamp. Two Sunday `/rwa/underlying-market` probes returned none either. The current documentation describes `referencePrice` as derived from token price. [EXP-RWA-010](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-010/results.json) therefore separates token-price freshness, market-state review and reference timestamp availability and reports **zero observations of independent reference age**. The Sunday probes also returned `openState=true` for NVDA, while one provider reported `offhours` and the other null. See the [market-state repro](../devex/repros/2026-10-04-underlying-market-weekend-state.md). Any off-hours price-discovery or predictive claim needs a separate, independently timed benchmark.

The first collector version measured token age against cycle start and could clamp a negative result to zero. The [clock correction](../devex/repros/2026-10-04-token-age-clock.md) preserves those append-only rows and recomputes every analyzed age from the row's `observed_at` and raw `tokenPriceUpdatedAt`. The corrected token-age medians at 11:05 UTC were 1.992 seconds for `offhours` rows and 5.005 seconds for rows with missing status. Those groups have different provider composition; neither number describes underlying-reference age.

At the [11:40 UTC Sunday checkpoint](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-004/results.json), the tape held 556 unique LIVE observations. All 40 sampled contracts had at least ten observations and changed token-price timestamps. The first nine contracts entered at 10:22 UTC and the other 31 at 10:43 UTC, so their full observed windows differ. To compare like with like, the [aligned-window table](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-004/common_window_ranges.csv) uses only the twelve complete five-minute slots from 10:45 through 11:40 UTC, with a maximum one-minute observation-time spread inside each slot. The median token-price min-to-max range across the 40 contracts in those same slots was 0.0903%. No independent underlying price, Monday opening result or completed trade was observed. The missing Saturday hours remain unfilled.

The [share-ratio audit](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-002/results.json) found 34 stock tickers present at both signed providers. No pair in this snapshot had a raw price gap above 10%. Several Ondo tokens have ratios far from one: KLAC's live catalog ratio was about 10.026 shares per token and its displayed token price was about $20,801, while its derived per-share price was about $2,074.70. The field's stated ratio semantics explain that arithmetic; they don't prove underlying rights or a tradeable spread. EXP-RWA-011 uses synthetic token split, stock split, rebase and halt cases to check that a policy doesn't mistake denomination changes for profit.

The per-contract [ratio arithmetic table](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-002/share_ratio_audit.csv) now exposes both clocks: the ratio came from the 11:00 UTC catalog snapshot, while the latest available token price came from a later collector row. Its `mixed_time_arithmetic_not_equivalence` state is deliberately narrower than a same-cycle price comparison. The separate signed-provider pair table uses prices and ratios from the same catalog response; even that arithmetic doesn't verify legal equivalence.

## Quote-size ladder

The corrected [EXP-RWA-009 ladder](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-009/quote_depth.csv) queried 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000 USDC into MSTRB and NVDAB at 10:36 UTC. All eight calls returned one LiquidMesh `SWAP` quote. The returned `fromToken.decimal=18` and echoed raw amount matched the intended size in every row. Reported `priceImpactPercent` remained below 0.013% in this sample. The output is an estimate with a short quote life; it doesn't establish trade completion, wallet eligibility, final gas cost or sell-side liquidity. The first ladder used the wrong BSC USDC decimals and is retained only as [DevEx counterevidence](../devex/repros/2026-10-04-bsc-usdc-decimals.md).

## Cross-provider check

The [EXP-RWA-008 table](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-008/cross_issuer_quotes.csv) used exactly 100 USDT per request at 10:37 UTC across bStock, Ondo and public-list xStock contracts for NVDA and MSTR. Binance returned one quote for NVDAB, NVDAon and MSTRB. MSTRon, MSTRx and NVDAx returned business code `40374` (`rwa_no_vendor_liquidity`) for this API, pair, size and time. That code doesn't prove absence of liquidity on every venue.

On the API's stated token/share ratios, the two NVDA buy quotes imply about 234.81 USDT per mathematical share for bStock and 238.44 USDT for Ondo, an indicative gross difference of 1.55%. These calls were sequential, not atomic. Fees, gas, sell route, redemption rights, issuer eligibility and legal equivalence aren't established. The difference is **not an arbitrage result**. A cross-issuer router is also not a selected product: existing projects already cover adjacent discovery and routing. This experiment only tells the policy engine when provider and route distinctions matter.

## Product consequence

The strongest confirmed implementation lesson is to keep three boundaries explicit: a listing or returned price row may contain no usable price or route; a quote may succeed for the wrong raw-unit size; a token-derived `referencePrice` isn't an independent stock-market clock. A deterministic policy can fail closed on these conditions. A user benefit and product differentiation still need proof before integrating that policy into the app.

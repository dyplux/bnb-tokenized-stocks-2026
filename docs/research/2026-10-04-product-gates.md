# Product gates after the first live Sunday measurements

**Decision time:** 4 October 2026, 13:55 UTC. This is a research decision, not a claim of user demand or a final submission concept. The earlier [1 October scorecard](idea-scorecard.md) predates the live API and route observations.

## What the evidence changed

The signed catalog exposed 488 BNB Chain representations, and a separate public list exposed 130 xStock listings. Their 492 ticker-and-type groups are **identity candidates**, not verified equivalent securities. The [catalog audit](2026-10-04-live-rwa-catalog-and-quotes.md) found 77 of 130 xStock price rows with no price or update time. The five-minute collector now samples 40 selected contracts: all 35 bStock equities and five Ondo names. The initial nine were a deliberate ticker filter, not an API cap. Broadening to every xStock would mainly repeat old or empty price rows at this point.

At a 100 USDT size on Sunday, the [route probe](2026-10-04-weekend-route-coverage.md) returned 38 routes from 40 requests. A closed US stock market therefore didn't mean the sampled stock-token routes were closed. A returned route didn't establish legal eligibility, an executable fill or profit. The [fixed-block audit](2026-10-04-bstock-multiplier-onchain.md) matched the catalog's current share multiplier on all 35 bStocks, but found no scheduled changes or observed split. The [clock source audit](2026-10-04-reference-clock-source-gate.md) found no independent issuer stock-reference timestamp in the observed signed payloads. Token price updates remain a different clock.

## Three remaining product hypotheses

| Candidate and user action | Supporting observation | Strongest objection | Promote only if | Stop if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Off-hours intelligence:** a holder decides whether to act before Monday's stock-market reopen. | The 40-contract weekend tape has live, changing token prices and a [locked Monday comparison protocol](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-004/monday-open-protocol.md). | No Monday result, verified underlying clock, funded fill or net economic edge. Earlier 55-weekend public Spot screen was near chance on simple direction. | The pre-registered Monday result shows a useful decision under an independent dated benchmark, and amount-specific routes survive cost and eligibility checks. | Predictive result is weak, unstable or unavailable at an executable size. |
| **B. Bounded mandate:** an agent developer asks whether a specific token-stock trade may proceed. | The [on-chain multiplier check](2026-10-04-bstock-multiplier-onchain.md) and [unfunded simulation](../devex/repros/2026-10-04-unsigned-build-simulation.md) show independent facts a deterministic guard can use. | [OneTicker](2026-10-04-gateway-substitute-audit.md) already offers a generic gate and off-hours tape. Issuer eligibility and reference age are unknown. The current policy has no execution authority. | A narrow, repeated developer task produces a different actionable result from an incumbent, with verified access, quote, simulation and receipt. | It only restates `UNKNOWN` or duplicates an existing pre-trade gate. |
| **C. First-trade recovery:** a new BNB Chain buyer enters a budget and learns why a chosen representation can or can't be acquired. | The [Sunday 40-contract probe](2026-10-04-weekend-route-coverage.md) produced two amount-specific liquidity failures; the [quote/build/simulation repro](../devex/repros/2026-10-04-unsigned-build-simulation.md) showed that API success can still predict a failed transaction. | PancakeSwap, Stock Hub and wallet flows already show quotes and errors. An unfunded wallet isn't a representative user's failed trade. | A real eligible buyer's same-task walkthrough identifies a missing recovery action and completes it more reliably than the incumbent flow. | The incumbent already resolves the error, or the user can't lawfully access the asset. |

**Current call:** retain the shared data and deterministic safety primitives; select no final product today. The highest-value next observations are the scheduled 30-minute `/underlying-market` watch, a regular-session comparison on 5 October, a verified access path and a user task that an incumbent fails. The watch must leave `reference_price_updated_at=null`, `reference_age_seconds=null` and `reference_age_status=UNKNOWN` unless an independent source and its timestamp semantics are actually observed. The unsigned simulation's `FAILED` result is evidence about that unfunded address only. Neither the on-chain multiplier match nor 38 returned quotes authorizes a trade.

# Bounded public scan for the NVDAB cash task

**Checked:** 2026-10-03 UTC. **Question:** is there observable public evidence that an NVDAB holder needs to choose between selling part of the token and borrowing USDT against it? This is a bounded source scan, not an interview or a measure of the whole market. No API quota, wallet, transaction or third-party contact was used.

## Sources and observations

- [P] The [Venus Core listing proposal](https://community.venus.io/t/bnb-chain-list-vtslab-vnvdab-and-vspcxb-markets-in-the-venus-core-pool/5832), published 2026-06-16 and checked 2026-10-03, set NVDAB's initial collateral factor at 60% and its own borrow cap at zero. The zero cap is for borrowing **NVDAB as the debt asset**. It does not by itself forbid supplying NVDAB as collateral and borrowing USDT from another market. This proposal is historical; the current cap and route must be read at a current block.
- [P] The [Venus July risk report](https://community.venus.io/t/venus-monthly-report-july-2026/5896), checked 2026-10-03, reported about $0.2 million RWA supply in its appendix across NVDAB, TSLAB, SPCXB, SKHYB and gold. Its own summary also says SKHYB alone reached about $700,000 across 38 suppliers by late July. Those two figures conflict on their face. The [August report](https://community.venus.io/t/venus-monthly-report-august-2026/5941), checked the same day, reported about $0.3 million RWA supply in its appendix. Until the classification or snapshot mismatch is resolved, none of these values is a defensible bStock market-size estimate. Their zero RWA debt column measures borrowing of RWA assets; it cannot show whether a holder borrowed USDT against RWA collateral.
- [S] A [mirror of a public post](https://zamantika.com/zh/xiaojianjian567/status/2097888717650464902), read 2026-10-03, reproduces a first-person claim by `@SharkBtcEth` of depositing NVDAB on Venus and borrowing USDT for a PancakeSwap LP. The original post and transaction weren't verified. The described purpose is a yield loop, not an observed need for spending cash or a sell-versus-borrow decision. Treat it as a lead only.
- [S] A [Binance Square post](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/359824715308342), checked 2026-10-03, describes NVDAB-backed USDT borrowing in the new bStocks DeFi Center with a conditional `if you don't want to sell` example. It doesn't show that the author holds NVDAB, borrowed USDT or faced a real cash decision. It is evidence of a public product narrative and an incumbent path, not of user demand. See the [substitute check](2026-10-03-binance-bstocks-defi-substitute.md).
- [?] Bounded searches on 2026-10-03 for `"NVDAB" "borrow" "USDT" user`, `"bStocks" "borrow" "sell" holder`, `"NVDAB" "Venus" borrow user`, `site:reddit.com "bStocks" "borrow"`, and their Chinese first-person variants produced no primary, wallet-verifiable request for this exact sell-versus-borrow task. This says what the search found, not that no such user exists.

## Consequence for D-017

The published reports establish that tokenized-stock collateral exists on Venus, but their conflicting category figures don't supply a reliable market size. They don't validate the specific cash-choice job. The mirror suggests one possible different reason to borrow USDT: reinvesting it in DeFi, but the claimed transaction isn't verified. An eligible consenting holder session must ask for the participant's actual recent objective and intended use before presenting a cash-target prompt. If the need is yield or leverage, classify it separately rather than treating it as demand for the D-017 cash choice. The D-033 stop rule and 2026-10-04 12:00 UTC checkpoint are unchanged.

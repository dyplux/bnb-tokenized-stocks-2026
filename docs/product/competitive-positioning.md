# Praeva competitive positioning

**Updated:** 2026-10-06. **Scope:** category boundaries for the current Tokenized Stocks build. This compares product roles, not specific vendors or their quality. Adjacent agent-security products exist; Praeva doesn't claim category novelty or universal uniqueness.

Praeva's bounded claim is an **RWA-specific pre-signing evidence and authorization model**. A proposed BNB Chain tokenized-equity action is bound to a contract, amount and mandate. The policy separates token-price time from an independently timed underlying reference, checks provider and market state, share scaling, route and impact, eligibility evidence and simulation state, then returns `ALLOW`, `DENY` or `NEED_HUMAN` with a SHA-256 receipt. The [policy](../../app/rwa_policy.py) implements those branches. The [dated safety report](../research/2026-10-04-safety-evidence.md) and [observed receipts](../submission/safety-judge-run.md) show why they matter. The observed outcomes are `NEED_HUMAN` and `DENY`; the green `ALLOW` is synthetic.

| Category | What it does | Where it overlaps | What Praeva adds in this build | What Praeva doesn't replace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Generic agent firewall or policy engine | Constrains an agent's actions using rules, permissions or approvals. | A rule can stop an action before execution. | Tokenized-equity fields, distinct token and underlying clocks, BNB Chain multiplier checks, quote and holder-access separation, and a dated receipt for one exact action. The [three-clock audit](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-010/results.json) and [multiplier audit](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-011/onchain_multiplier_audit.json) ground these checks. | General agent permissions, prompt protection, tool-call controls or broader organizational policy. |
| Smart wallet or signer | Holds signing authority and applies transaction or spend controls. | Both sit before a transaction is signed. | A separate evidence decision about the specific RWA representation, market session, route, eligibility, simulation and mandate. The [architecture](../knowledge/technical-architecture.md) keeps the policy read-only. | Custody, key management, transaction signing, chain broadcast or wallet-level limits. |
| Tokenized-stock router | Finds a route or quoted execution path for an asset and amount. | Both inspect the intended asset and a route. | Treats a route as one input, then checks reference provenance, access, economic scaling, impact, simulation and mandate. An observed route still yielded [`NEED_HUMAN`](../judge/observed-unsafe.json); a quoted 100 USDT request under a 20 USDT cap yielded [`DENY`](../judge/observed-mandate-deny.json). | Route discovery, best execution, liquidity aggregation or transaction settlement. The [gateway substitute audit](../research/2026-10-04-gateway-substitute-audit.md) records the existing gateway category. |
| Trading agent | Chooses and may execute actions under a strategy. | It can ask Praeva whether one proposed action meets a mandate. | A deterministic decision independent of an LLM's preference, including an explicit `NEED_HUMAN` when required evidence is absent. | Signal generation, portfolio strategy, returns or trade execution. The [Monday result](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-004/monday-findings.md) was `SAFETY_ONLY`, with no predictive edge claim. |
| Compliance or access layer | Determines legal, issuer or user permissions. | Eligibility is a required input to a safe action. | Keeps access `UNKNOWN` until a dated, caller-verified basis is supplied; a successful quote doesn't set it to eligible. See the [issuer access gate](../research/2026-10-04-issuer-access-gate.md) and [eligibility guard](../../app/rwa_policy.py). | KYC, jurisdictional review, issuer terms, legal advice or an eligibility decision. No individual's eligibility has been verified here. |
| Simulation service | Predicts whether a constructed transaction may succeed. | Simulation state is one policy input. | Binds that state to identity, freshness, route, multiplier, access and mandate checks before an agent may act. The [linked pre-execution packet](pre-execution-packet-linked-2026-10-04.json) preserves an unsigned build and an unfunded simulation prediction of `FAILED`. | EVM simulation, gas estimation, contract auditing or execution proof. No passing funded simulation or stock-token trade is claimed. |

The combination is implemented for a narrow BNB Chain task. The [technical architecture](../knowledge/technical-architecture.md), [receipt algorithm](../../app/rwa_policy.py) and [judge guide](../../JUDGE.md) are the reproducible proof. Individual checks may exist elsewhere. Neither the observed decisions nor this comparison establish demand, market leadership or a defensible moat.

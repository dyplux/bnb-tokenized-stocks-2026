# Post-hold research report: 1 October 2026

I read the requested project files and `CORRECOES.md`. The prior pre-trade receipt/comparison idea remains rejected as a standalone direction. No code or files were changed.

The CLI didn't expose a reliable remaining five-hour allowance. No authenticated API calls or trades were made.

Documentation shows that issuers and protocols already solve many basic post-hold operations. The credible gaps are cross-system monitoring and recovery, not another explanation of token properties.

## Job 1: Recover from a corporate-action or redemption interruption

**Target person:** An eligible BNB Chain holder of bStocks or Ondo Stocks.

**Trigger:** A dividend, split, issuer-term change, market halt, or redemption pause changes the displayed position or blocks the holder’s intended next action.

**Before → after:** “I see that my stock-token balance, multiplier, or redemption status changed but cannot tell whether I must act; the product identifies the event, affected contract, current issuer status, and safe next step.”

**Fact evidence**

- Binance states that bStocks dividends and splits are handled through a multiplier; raw balances may remain unchanged while displayed balances change. Deposits, withdrawals, conversions, redemptions, and trading may be paused during processing. ([bStocks FAQ](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/faq/detail/f0c03cd6509a4085b4cce1636f16be38), published 11 June 2026)
- Binance says advance corporate-action notice is provided where practicable, but is not guaranteed. ([bStocks FAQ](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/faq/detail/f0c03cd6509a4085b4cce1636f16be38))
- Ondo automatically reinvests dividends and reflects splits in the token; minting and redemption may be paused around corporate actions, volatility, or risk controls. ([Ondo Stocks](https://ondo.finance/ondo-stocks), checked 27 September 2026)
- Ondo says eligible holders can redeem to stablecoins, but ineligible holders must continue holding or use a permitted secondary market. ([Ondo Stocks](https://ondo.finance/ondo-stocks))
- Robinhood’s comparison product exposes processed corporate actions, pending multiplier changes, trading halts, and timestamps through a dedicated API. ([Robinhood Stock Token APIs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-token-apis/), checked September 2026)
- Binance Web3 API exposes RWA token identity, issuer/platform, token-to-share ratio, token price, reference price, market status, next open/close times, and underlying dividend fields. It does not document a corporate-action event feed. ([RWA Data API](https://web3.binance.com/en/dev-docs/catalog/web3-wallet/api/rest-api/rwa-data))

**Is it already solved?** Partly.

Issuer accounting is solved or automated. Binance has issuer notices and multiplier semantics; Ondo has status information and automatic treatment. What is not demonstrated is a single BNB-facing recovery view that reconciles raw wallet balance, adjusted position, issuer status, contract eligibility, and the user’s available exit route across issuers.

**Existing substitutes:** Issuer FAQ/status pages, Binance announcements, wallet balance screens, block explorers, manual contract inspection, and simply waiting. Robinhood has a richer API model, but it is a different ecosystem.

**Indispensable BNB Chain + Binance Web3 API work:** BNB Chain is necessary to read the actual held contract and raw balance. Binance Web3 API would provide canonical issuer/contract mapping, token-to-share ratio, market status, and position valuation. It is not sufficient by itself for event detection; an issuer announcement/status source would also be required.

**Counterevidence:** bStocks explicitly says users need take no action for normal dividends and splits. Ondo also says holders need not claim anything. A recovery product may therefore solve only unusual cases, not ordinary ownership.

**Risk:** Incorrectly interpreting an issuer’s multiplier, eligibility, tax treatment, or pause could cause a user to trade or redeem at the wrong time. Legal terms and jurisdiction restrictions are material.

**Minimum no-funds demo:** A clearly labelled, read-only replay using public Binance/issuer documentation examples: show raw balance, multiplier-adjusted display balance, a simulated documented split/dividend event, issuer status, and “wait / sell on secondary market / redeem if eligible.” No transaction or authenticated API call.

**Direct falsifier:** In a same-task test, an ordinary holder can find the event, understand the balance change, determine eligibility, and choose the correct next action from issuer and wallet interfaces in under five minutes, without consulting a third-party tool.

**Distinct from Bell:** Yes, if limited to post-hold event recovery and action routing. It becomes Bell-like if it mainly compares representations, contracts, or pre-trade rights.

## Job 2: Know whether a tokenized-stock collateral position is safe to keep

**Target person:** A BNB Chain holder who has supplied bStocks as collateral on Venus or Lista and borrowed stablecoins.

**Trigger:** The stock price falls, the collateral multiplier changes, protocol parameters change, liquidity weakens, or the holder wants to unwind without liquidation.

**Before → after:** “I know I have borrowed against my stock exposure but cannot see the combined asset, protocol, and liquidation state; the product shows the position’s current health, the reason for deterioration, and the least-destructive action to restore safety.”

**Fact evidence**

- BNB Chain says bStocks can be supplied or used as collateral on Venus and Lista DAO. ([BNB Chain bStocks announcement](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/blog/introducing-bstocks-on-bnb-chain-trade-247-with-zero-fees-deploy-across-defi-protocols-with-full-self-custody), 11 June 2026)
- A later BNB Chain campaign announcement says Lista supported all six initial bStocks as collateral and Venus added supported bStock collateral markets. ([BNB Chain campaign](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/blog/win-a-share-of-300k-with-bstocks-on-trust-wallet-pancakeswap-aster-lista-dao-venus-and-native), 22 June 2026)
- Venus describes supplying BEP-20 assets as collateral and borrowing other assets; supported markets and risk parameters change through governance. ([Venus](https://www.venus-protocol.org/), checked 30 September 2026)
- Binance Web3 API documents address balances, portfolio overview, recent PnL, token-specific PnL, DeFi positions, protocol lists, and protocol investment details. ([Wallet and DeFi API](https://web3.binance.com/en/dev-docs/catalog/web3-wallet/api/rest-api/wallet-api))
- Binance’s RWA API provides token price, reference price, market status, issuer, ratio, and underlying market data, which could feed collateral valuation. ([RWA Data API](https://web3.binance.com/en/dev-docs/catalog/web3-wallet/api/rest-api/rwa-data))
- Base already markets the analogous post-hold use case: lend, borrow, or use tokenized stocks as collateral across Aave, Morpho, and other protocols. ([Base stocks](https://www.base.org/stocks), checked September 2026)

**Is it already solved?** The lending action is solved; cross-position monitoring is not proven solved.

Venus and Lista provide the lending markets. Wallets provide balances. Binance Web3 API provides portfolio and DeFi data. No reviewed source documents a user-facing cross-protocol alert that combines bStock price/multiplier changes, collateral factor, debt, liquidation distance, and an executable recovery sequence.

**Existing substitutes:** Venus/Lista dashboards, manual price checks, wallet portfolio views, protocol governance announcements, and spreadsheet calculations.

**Indispensable BNB Chain + Binance Web3 API work:** BNB Chain is essential because the collateral and lending positions are on BNB Chain. Binance Web3 API is useful for canonical RWA identity, current/reference pricing, wallet balances, portfolio PnL, and DeFi positions. The product is not credible without those live read-only inputs; static explanations would be decorative.

**Counterevidence:** Protocol interfaces already calculate collateral health. A holder who actively borrows may understand liquidation mechanics, and the incremental value of another dashboard may be small. The BNB Chain announcements are ecosystem marketing, not evidence of ordinary-user demand.

**Risk:** Liquidation thresholds, oracle behavior, market liquidity, token multiplier semantics, and protocol support can change. A stale API response could falsely signal safety. This is financial-risk software and would require prominent freshness and failure states.

**Minimum no-funds demo:** A read-only labelled fixture containing one documented bStock, one Venus/Lista-style collateral position, a price drop, and a multiplier change. Display data timestamps, “API unavailable” behavior, and a calculated warning without signing, borrowing, repaying, or broadcasting transactions.

**Direct falsifier:** Protocol dashboards and Binance Wallet together already show collateral health, debt, price freshness, and the correct recovery action clearly enough that test users make no faster or safer decision with the proposed view.

**Distinct from Bell:** Yes. The job is maintaining or unwinding an existing DeFi-backed position, not deciding whether two stock-token representations are comparable before purchase.

## Agent Studio assessment

I found no necessary Agent Studio use. Agent Studio provides persistent agent identity, ERC-8183 task commerce, and x402 payments, but this research task can be served by a normal read-only application using issuer data and Binance Web3 API. ([BNB Agent Studio](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/bnb-agent-studio); [Quickstart](https://docs.bnbchain.org/developer-kit/bnbchain-studio/quickstart/))

Persistence might become necessary for long-lived monitoring; identity and x402 would become necessary only if the monitor autonomously bought paid data or sold signed monitoring jobs. No reviewed source establishes that requirement today. Do not force the special prize.

## Recommendation

Advance only Job 2 to a falsification test, with Job 1 retained as a secondary research lead. It has the clearest indispensable Binance Web3 API role and a concrete post-hold consequence, liquidation or safe recovery, while remaining distinct from Bell.

Do not approve implementation yet. First test whether a real BNB collateral position is difficult to monitor across Venus/Lista, Binance Wallet, and the issuer’s data. Unknowns remain: live Binance API scopes and quotas, exact DeFi response fields, supported bStock markets at runtime, alert delivery reliability, and whether ordinary holders actually experience this problem.

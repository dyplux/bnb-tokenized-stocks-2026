## Recommendation and dissent

Observe one consented, eligible holder performing the same cash task in an incumbent trading venue and Venus before showing Dyplux's clearly hypothetical scenario. Record the action, elapsed time, missing information and whether the combined view changes a defensible decision. A compliment or preference isn't evidence of benefit. Stop if incumbents already give equal clarity, no participant can be observed promptly, personal Venus risk can't be made safe, or a signed sell quote is unavailable.

The coordinator retains D-017 provisionally while seeking that evidence. The reviewer dissents from treating it as the strongest or functional project now.

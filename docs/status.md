## Current decision

The old H1/H2/H3 scores were a subjective research ordering, not demand evidence. The founder challenged H1's similarity to Bell and rejected blocked-exit recovery as the main direction. [D-006](decisions/decision-log.md) prioritized a public event after cash close; [D-008](decisions/decision-log.md) narrowed the trigger to BTC-linked MSTRB because SEC filings cannot arrive on weekends. Binance Spot trades bStocks around the clock. yostocks, Portir, NightDesk and OneTicker cover much of the quote and session workflow. [D-010](decisions/decision-log.md) tested dividend cash-out, but [D-011](decisions/decision-log.md) retired it as the leading consumer idea. [D-014](decisions/decision-log.md) found little size-dependent impact in one liquid NVDAB pool; [D-016](decisions/decision-log.md) rejected a simple weekend direction claim. [D-017](decisions/decision-log.md) now authorizes a bounded, read-only sell-or-borrow slice because it asks a different cash decision using a live stock sell quote and Venus risk. No live Binance Web3 quote or observed user task exists, so the product isn't functional or validated. Binance documents `referencePrice` inconsistently; it isn't an independent TradFi quote in this product.

[D-007](decisions/decision-log.md) first proposed weekend access for an eligible person with BNB Chain funds. Binance's 24/7 Spot bStocks product and Ondo's six 24/7 direct assets weaken access as a standalone claim. The paid Agent Studio idea remains optional: no separate buyer for an event service has been identified. Binance Agentic Wallet already documents fractional selling and persistent trading rules. Wallet support pages already explain common trade failures. Venus had no active bStock protection-price divergence at the measured block, and the eleven-weekend Spot screen found no simple directional edge. The provisional cash decision must change a real action for an eligible holder and show its value against Steward, Portir, Venus and a trading venue.

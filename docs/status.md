## Current decision

The old H1/H2/H3 scores were a subjective research ordering, not demand evidence. The founder challenged H1's similarity to Bell and rejected blocked-exit recovery as the main direction. [D-006](decisions/decision-log.md) prioritized a public event after cash close; [D-008](decisions/decision-log.md) narrowed the trigger to BTC-linked MSTRB because SEC filings cannot arrive on weekends. Binance Spot trades bStocks around the clock. yostocks, Portir, NightDesk and OneTicker cover much of the quote and session workflow. [D-010](decisions/decision-log.md) tested dividend cash-out, but [D-011](decisions/decision-log.md) retires it as the leading consumer idea: the ordinary-holder amount looks small and the larger illustration comes from a daily inverse leveraged ETF. There is no live Binance Web3 quote or observed user task, so no implementation is approved. The `referencePrice` field is derived from token price and cannot be used as an independent TradFi benchmark.

[D-007](decisions/decision-log.md) first proposed weekend access for an eligible person with BNB Chain funds. Binance's 24/7 Spot bStocks product and Ondo's six 24/7 direct assets weaken access as a standalone claim. The paid Agent Studio idea remains optional: no separate buyer for an event service has been identified. Binance Agentic Wallet already documents fractional selling and persistent trading rules. The next candidate has to change a real action for an eligible small wallet and show its value against these existing tools.

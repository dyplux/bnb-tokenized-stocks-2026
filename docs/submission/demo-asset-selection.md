## Decision

**Canonical technical preflight asset: NVDAB, the NVDA bStock representation.** It has the fullest observed chain from a 10 USDT quote to an unsigned build and an off-chain simulation at the existing demo address. Its current bStock multiplier also matched an on-chain read at a fixed block. This choice removes NVDAon as the only route for the demo, but it **doesn't clear a mainnet trade**. We haven't established that the founder and the chosen self-custodial route are eligible. The demo stays read-only until that access gate, the funded simulation and a specific human approval pass.

No candidate currently meets every requirement for a **legitimately usable, funded** execution. The selection above is a technical target for the next bounded check, not a recommendation to buy it. If bStock access can't be verified, don't force NVDAB: test an xStock with a current BNB Chain route and its asset-specific terms, or keep the public demo in `NEED_HUMAN`.

## Approval and evidence gate

Before a live trade, obtain a dated issuer or venue basis for the founder's jurisdiction and this asset, inspect the exact approval target and allowance, fund only the stated small amount plus BNB gas, and rerun the **same wallet and quote** through build and passing simulation. The policy must return `ALLOW` on the same proposed action. Only then prepare a packet with source and destination contracts, maximum spend, expected output, slippage, gas ceiling, quote expiry, receipt hash and recovery plan for the founder's explicit approval. A new quote after approval requires new approval. No signer or broadcast path is active today.

## Fallback rule

If NVDAB access remains unknown, keep the judge path on a dated NVDAB safety finding and show why it says `NEED_HUMAN`. The 4 October [wrapped xStock probe](../research/2026-10-04-xstock-route-fallback.md) found two indexed USDT pairs but no 10 USDT Binance Web3 routes, so those wrappers don't currently remove the demo blocker. A synthetic safe policy fixture may demonstrate what an `ALLOW` receipt looks like, labelled synthetic throughout; it isn't evidence of a funded trade. The final submission must say whether the live action was completed, blocked or never approved.

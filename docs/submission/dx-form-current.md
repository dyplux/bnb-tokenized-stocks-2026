## What we actually built and called

The current read-only safety screen uses signed **RWA Data** (`/tokens`, `/price`, `/underlying-market`) and **Trading** (`/aggregator/quote`) for one NVDA action. Its bStock branch also reads a BNB Chain fixed-block multiplier. The Ondo branch calls the public endpoint documented by Binance's tokenized-securities Wallet Skill; the skill package itself wasn't installed or invoked, and the observed response had no independent stock-feed timestamp. A separate **Transaction API** research path built 11 unsigned `SWAP` transactions and submitted ten off-chain simulations by 17:25 UTC. These simulation API calls returned business code zero, while the predicted unfunded transactions failed. No transaction was signed or broadcast. The official Agentic Wallet runtime was not installed: its [install guide](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/agentic-wallet/quickstart/install-agentic-wallet) requires a Binance account and MPC wallet, which the founder reports are unavailable.

## Numbers and limits

At the 17:25 UTC snapshot, the log had **484 calls across 13 endpoint/method groups**: 424 signed Binance Web3 calls, eight public Binance reads, 50 BNB Chain RPC calls and two timed-out xStocks public reads. Signed `/tokens` had 131 business successes, 2,193.37 ms median and 3,875.75 ms nearest-rank p95 observed latency. Signed `/price` had 126 business successes and one HTTP 414. Signed `/quote` had 108 HTTP-200 responses: 97 business successes, two `40367` and nine `40374` rejections. All 11 unsigned `/swap` calls and ten `/simulate` API calls returned business success. No HTTP 429 appears in this snapshot. These are mixed collector and exploratory calls, not a controlled benchmark. The [40-contract Sunday tape](../../experiments/EXP-RWA-010/results.json) tests token-price freshness only; its independent stock-reference age remains `UNKNOWN`. No observed spread is a realised profit or fill.

Our errors must stay separate: an early quote ladder used six-decimal USDC units for an 18-decimal BSC token, and an early collector version calculated token age from cycle start. Both were corrected and retained in the [DevEx evidence summary](../devex/2026-10-04-evidence-summary.md). At 15:10 the detached collector also skipped a scheduling boundary when a successful call finished after the next slot began; health caught it, we fixed scheduling from the completed slot and observed 40 rows in the 15:15 slot.
